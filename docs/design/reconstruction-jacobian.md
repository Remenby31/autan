# The reconstruction's gradients in the approximate Jacobian of viscous meshes

Design for #395 (note 1 of 2: the flow's model; note 2, the coupled SA solve, follows #137 and the RAE 2822 grid), written before any code by CFD - Euler and CFD - Multigrille. The steady implicit step solves (V/Δt + J̃) ΔU = −R(U) with J̃ the assembled approximate Jacobian: every face's dF/dW with the reconstruction's gradients and the limiter held fixed ([euler.md](euler.md#time-stepping)). This note adds, in the shared convective assembly and only where `NavierStokes` asks for it, the convective flux's dependence on the cells' reconstruction gradients through their least-squares weights.

## Problem, measured

TMR flat plate 69×49 (#395, `physics/ns-sa` c3966068): once the SER raises the CFL number past ~10³ at second order, the SA plate locks into a period-2 cycle (full steps, solves within tolerance, residual at −3 to −4); the laminar plate wanders without converging; with ν̃ and μ_t frozen inside the cycle, the flow wanders as the laminar one does (#395, isolation diagnostic). At a state of the cycle, ‖(J − J̃) v‖ / ‖J v‖ is 10⁻⁹–10⁻² at first order and, at second order, 0.15–0.25 over the domain and 0.38–0.70 on the wall cells' density and energy rows (central differences of R, three random v, ε 10⁻⁵ and 10⁻⁷). Split into convective and viscous parts at the same state, the gap is convective: ‖g_c‖ / ‖g‖ = 0.995–1.000 in every row group, on the wall ring and over the domain; the viscous blocks, the boundary fit's included, carry at most 0.7 % (#395). The missing part is the convective flux's dependence on the reconstruction's gradients, which J̃ holds fixed and which the wall cells' aspect ratio (10³–10⁴) makes large. With the true Jacobian applied matrix-free (#397), the laminar plate converged in 1655 steps.

## Why

The second-order face state is w_f = w_c + φ_c ⊙ (∇w_c · r_cf), with ∇w_c = Σ_j W_cj (w_j − w_c) the least-squares fit over the cell's stencil (`stencil_lsq_weight`, face neighbours and their neighbours) and φ the limiter, componentwise. J̃ differentiates F(w_L,f, w_R,f) with respect to w_L and w_R only through the first term: ∂w_f/∂w_c = I. The fit adds ∂w_f/∂w_j = diag(φ_c) (W_cj · r_cf) for every stencil cell j and −diag(φ_c) Σ_j (W_cj · r_cf) on w_c. On an isotropic cell |W_cj · r_cf| is of order ½ for the cell across the face, so the omission is a fraction of the term kept. In a wall cell stretched 10³–10⁴, the fit's weights along the wall normal fall on the cells above and below a cell of height h, while the faces' r runs along the wall over lengths up to 10³–10⁴ h. Then |W_cj · r_cf| is no longer bounded by ½, and the omitted part can exceed the kept one. That is what #395 measures: 38–70 % of J v missing on the wall cells' density and energy rows. Continuity carries no viscous flux at all, and the split of #395 puts 99.5–100 % of the gap in the convective part on every row group, energy included.

Two choices keep this from becoming #397:
- **The operator stays the assembled J̃**: the solve's operator and the preconditioner, with these entries added. It is not the matrix-free Jacobian, and nothing depends on a perturbed state.
- **φ and the reconstruction's positivity fallback stay held at U**, exactly as J̃ already holds them. This note adds the gradients' dependence only: no derivative through the limiter's minimum and maximum, or through the fallback's branch.

#397's loss on the limited Euler cases came with a model that changed both the operator (matrix-free) and how much of the true derivative it followed. This one only fills in a smooth part of the linear model, the least-squares fit, which is linear in w. There is still no a priori guarantee that the step stays as robust. The new entries move weight off the diagonal and can reduce the blocks' diagonal dominance, which the multigrid and the line smoother rely on. Hence the gate reports the linear drops, cycles and failures before and after, and the Euler cases stay bit for bit (Scope).

## Form

**The chain.** For an interior face f = (L, R) and each side s ∈ {L, R} whose reconstruction is not the cell value (the fallback held as in J̃), the generated face Jacobian already gives A_s = ∂F/∂w_s,f (5 × 5, the `*_faces_jacobian` loops, gradients and φ held). The added blocks, times dW/dU of the columns' cells as J̃'s blocks are, are:
- for every column cell j of the side's gradient: A_s diag(φ_s) (Ŵ_sj · r_sf), into row L with the face's sign (+) and row R with its sign (−);
- on column s: −A_s diag(φ_s) Σ_j (Ŵ_sj · r_sf).

Boundary faces add the same terms to their owner's row, A from `*_boundaries_jacobian`. These are assembly-time products of stored blocks: no residual evaluation, no generated loop changes. The warped-face corrections (`warp`, `wall_warp`) depend on the gradients directly and stay out of the chain: they vanish on planar faces (the plate's faces are planar: their moment M is zero), and elsewhere this is an approximation added to J̃'s, declared here and measured by the gate's (J − J̃) v.

**Which weights.** The block matrix has a 5 × 5 block for a cell and each of its face neighbours, and nothing else. The stencil's own weights W reach the face neighbours' neighbours. Truncating them to the pattern drops 42 % of Σ |W_sj · r_sf| over the wall ring's faces and 52 % over the domain. It also leaves a gradient that no longer reproduces a linear field (Σ_j W_cj (x_j − x_c) ≠ I once entries are removed). The chain therefore uses its own fit Ŵ, a least-squares gradient over the cell's face neighbours only, weighted by the inverse squared distance and solved in the subspace the face neighbours span. With d_cj = x_j − x_c (components within 64 ulps of the coordinates set to zero, as the stencil's fit does, euler.md) and e_cj = d_cj / |d_cj|: Ŵ_cj = B_c⁺ e_cj / |d_cj|, with B_c = Σ_j e_cj e_cjᵀ. The weighting keeps the fit exact for linear fields: Σ_j Ŵ_cj d_cjᵀ = B_c⁺ B_c, the identity on the span. It also makes B_c dimensionless and independent of the cells' sizes and orientation: its eigenvalues are sums of squared direction cosines, of order 1 along every direction the neighbours span, however stretched or rotated the cell. A cutoff on B_c's eigenvalues is then a test of geometric rank, not of scale. B_c⁺ is the pseudo-inverse from the symmetric eigen-decomposition of the 3 × 3 matrix, with eigenvalues below 10⁻¹⁰ of the largest set to zero. The cell's gradient then has no component along a direction its face neighbours do not span: the slab's thickness on the plate, which the residual's own fit takes from the boundary samples and whose derivative J̃ therefore keeps omitting. Unit tests:
- a linear field reproduced exactly on the four test meshes and on the plate's wall cells (aspect ratio up to 10⁴);
- Codex's counterexample: offsets ±(1, 0, 0), ±(0, 10⁻⁶, 0), ±(0, 0, 1), and the same rotated by 30° about z, where w = y must give the gradient (0, 1, 0).

Ŵ lies entirely in row s's pattern. What it still cannot place are the cross terms: row t (the other side) of a face receives column j only when j is t or one of t's face neighbours. Measured on the 69×49 plate mesh, as a geometric quantity before any run (Σ over faces and both rows of |Ŵ_sj · r_sf| with the column outside the pattern, against all terms, with Ŵ as defined above): 18.5 % on the wall ring's faces, 24.5 % over the domain. On that mesh Ŵ reproduces a linear field to 4.4 × 10⁻¹⁶ in every cell. Ŵ is computed once at setup: 2D-or-3D like the stencil's (the plate is one cell thick: the offsets' components within 64 ulps are zero, euler.md), stored 3 per face incidence (3 × 2 F_interior doubles), freed with the matrix.

**The parameter.** `Scheme::reconstruction_jacobian` (default false): `Euler::assemble` adds the blocks above when set. `NavierStokes` sets it, and `EulerPhysics` does not. The code is written once, in `Euler::Impl`, where the face blocks are assembled; #409 would set it for Euler.

**Cost.** Setup: one 3 × 3 symmetric eigen-decomposition per cell (of B_c) and 3 doubles per face incidence. Per assembly, the terms are accumulated in primitive variables. Per face side and column: one 5 × 5 times diagonal-5 product, added into a primitive accumulator of the destination block (row, column). Then each destination block is converted once to conserved variables by the column cell's dW/dU, a full 5 × 5 product per block. This is about the number of existing off-diagonal blocks, plus one per diagonal, not one per contribution. Measured in the gate as CPU time per step against the branch without it.

## Scope

`NavierStokes` sets the parameter; `EulerPhysics` does not, so every Euler run is bit for bit what `main` gives (the Euler physics' assembly is untouched). The same terms for Euler, where the limited cases are, are #409, with their own design and gate. The multigrid, the line smoother and the steady step are unchanged: the new entries land in the existing face blocks; the hierarchy and the Galerkin coarse operators take them as they take today's.

## Two candidates, declared before the probe measurement

A probe found, before any production code, that the Form's first statement (columns outside the pattern dropped, the full −Σ c kept on column s) makes J̃ worse: it no longer maps a uniform δW to zero, and its error grows to 0.525 on tets against 0.120 without the chain (Euler probe, #395 thread). Two candidates replace it, each justified independently of the plate, both measured by the declared threshold below and both recorded whatever they give:

- **(a) lump**: in each row, the side's term keeps only the columns in that row's pattern, and the −Σ on column s runs over the kept columns only; a dropped δW_j is replaced by δW_s. Justification: a uniform δW gives exactly zero, as the true derivative does, on any mesh.
- **(b) lump and boundary samples**: (a), with Ŵ taking a cell's boundary samples exactly when the residual's fit W does (a non-zero stencil weight; on a one-cell-thick mesh W takes every boundary face). A sample's value depends on its own cell only, so its term lands on column s, in the pattern. Justification: on the columns it can place, the Jacobian's fit uses the same samples as the residual's, i.e. consistency with the discretisation, not a fit to the plate.

Declared reading for each, by the threshold of the gate (‖(J − J̃) v‖ / ‖J v‖ on the wall ring's continuity rows at the laminar plate's step 1000, #395's protocol, at most 0.5 times its value without the chain): at or below → the candidate passes; above → it fails. If neither passes, this note fails as declared within the pattern (the probable remainder: W's second ring, outside the pattern), recorded in #395, and the next step (a pattern extended to the second ring, or a generated boundary Jacobian per condition kind, E1004 today) goes back to the orchestrator with its cost. Probe measurements, before any production code: both candidates on a branch never merged, the boundary samples' derivative by central differences of the boundary states.
